# Security

For confidential or security-sensitive concerns, use the private contact listed
in the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not include sensitive
information in a public issue.

Include the affected skill or file, the possible impact, and enough information
for the maintainers to understand the concern.

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/siemens/tia-portal-ai-extensions/issues)
for ordinary questions, bugs, and improvement suggestions.
